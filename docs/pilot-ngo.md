## 1. Pilot goal

Validate with real use in **Phases 3 and 4**:

- Animal listing and adoption flow (request → review → completion)
- Occurrence registration and follow-up in the operating region
- Feedback on usability (fast listing) and compatibility-score relevance

---

## 2. Selection criteria

Prefer an NGO that:

- Operates in a clear geography (city/neighborhoods) aligned with the pilot launch
- Can list a few real animals and respond to requests
- Will give structured feedback (biweekly call or async)
- Can provide docs for `verified` (CNPJ / bylaws) when that flow exists
- Accepts that the product is evolving (bugs and changes expected)

**Not required** at first contact: legacy integrations, high volume, multi-city coverage.

---

## 3. What the NGO tests / effort / gain

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Tests | Animal listing, request management, occurrence view in area |
| Estimated effort | ~1–2 h/week early pilot (listings + feedback) |
| Gains | Animal visibility, traceable adoption process, regional occurrence map, product voice |

---

## 4. Feedback channel (default)

**Fixed default:** **biweekly** 30-min call + async channel (WhatsApp or email) for ad-hoc questions.

Suggested call agenda: what worked · what blocked · 1 priority for the next two weeks.
