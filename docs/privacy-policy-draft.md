## 2. What data we collect

Depending on use, we may process:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, password (hashed), phone, city/state, profile type (`guardian`, NGO, clinic, public agency, biologist) |
| Tax IDs | `taxId` / `companyTaxId` when required (adoption request or institutional signup) |
| Adoption preferences | Space, time, experience, size/species prefs |
| Animals & adoptions | Animal info, status, adoption history |
| Occurrences | Description, type, **location**, photos, status; with or without an account (anonymous) |
| Waitlist | Name, email, profile type, optional city/state, consent timestamp |
| Technical | Security logs (e.g. abuse attempts) to protect the service |

---

## 3. Why we use data

- Create and manage your account
- Enable search, matching, and adoption requests
- Record and show occurrences on the map for response and public interest
- Notify about adoption and occurrence status
- Produce **aggregated indicators** (neighborhood/city) without identifying people
- Meet legal duties and prevent fraud/abuse
- Operate the waitlist before full product launch

---

## 4. Geolocation and photos

- Occurrence location supports map display and regional NGO/agency response.
- Submitting an occurrence authorizes use of location and photos for those purposes.
- **Exports and analytics dashboards** never disclose exact coordinates — only **neighborhood or city aggregates**.
- We strip identifiable image metadata (e.g. EXIF) when technically feasible.

---

## 5. Sharing

We share data only as needed to run the service:

- With participating **NGOs, clinics, and agencies** per flow (e.g. adoption review, occurrence validation)
- With **infrastructure providers** (hosting, file storage, email) under confidentiality obligations
- In **aggregated, anonymized** form for research and public interest

We do not sell personal data.

---

## 6. Retention and deletion

| Situation | Policy |
|---|---|
| Active account | Kept while the account exists |
| Account deletion request | Account made inaccessible; personal data purged within **90 days** (including backup alignment where feasible) |
| Soft delete | Immediate `deletedAt`; hard PII purge at T+90d |
| Collective-interest records | Occurrences/animals may remain **anonymized** (no identity link). Photos may stay **without author metadata** |
